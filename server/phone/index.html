Another Me

Send a photo to the screen

Choose a photo from your phone that says something about you. It will appear on the screen in front of you, where you can still decide whether to use it.

Before sending, your phone makes a smaller copy and removes its location and camera details. The copy passes through the Another Me server only to reach the screen and is deleted from the server as soon as the screen receives it, or after 10 minutes. Nothing else on your phone is accessed.
